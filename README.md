### 03 — Hybrid Crypto Bank  
*Fiat/crypto platform & payment routing*

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chidioffor/chidioffor/master/assets/hybrid-bank.svg" alt="Hybrid Crypto Bank visual with payment rail architecture" width="100%" />

| Focus | Engineering signals |
|---|---|

`ACH` · `Wire` · `SEPA` · `SWIFT` · `Faster Payments` · `Card payments` · `Mobile money` · `Local bank transfer` · `Internal transfers`

> **Implementation status:** Stripe Treasury connected-account and onboarding work is implemented. The additional rails above are represented as provider-routing and integration paths; they are not presented here as production-enabled services.

<details>
<summary><strong>What this work demonstrates</strong></summary>

- Decomposing a broad hybrid banking product into distinct, understandable responsibility areas.

- Designing architecture that can grow without turning product complexity into a monolith.
</details>
